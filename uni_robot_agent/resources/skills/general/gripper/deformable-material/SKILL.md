# Track the material region, not just the hand

Use `general/SKILL.md` for attachment checks. Identify the region to move and
the region or support it should meet from the current instruction and visible
shape. An overall compact silhouette does not establish their relationship.

Choose exposed material with clearance to pinch the intended region rather
than the support or a neighboring layer. During a small test motion, distinguish
that region following the hand from the entire object sliding or lifting.
If the whole material moves, reassess support and contact before treating the
motion as a local deformation.

Plan a path that brings the selected region to its target without reopening
previously established relationships. Release into support, withdraw and compare
the same landmarks in the settled shape. The shape held by the fingers may not
persist after release.

Re-identify accessible regions after sliding, bunching or deformation before
choosing another grasp. A previously visible corner or edge need not remain at
the same coordinate or on the accessible layer.

With two holds, inspect which material each hand controls and whether motion
creates the intended shape or pulls the holds against one another. If either
slips or the relationship becomes unclear, restore observable support and
replan; handling one region at a time is an option, not a universal requirement.

Choose corrections from the observed displaced, extended or bunched regions.
Check the requested relationships after release rather than counting grasps or
using hand endpoints as evidence of the final shape.
